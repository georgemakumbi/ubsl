Technical Pedigree

Why Banks Trust Our Engineers

At Unique Business Systems Limited, we recognize that when a currency sorter or cheque scanner breaks down, it stalls transaction loops and increases teller vulnerability. Our support department is engineered to prevent this.

Every technician in our field team is factory-trained or certified, possessing extensive knowledge of the community banking and financial institution industry. We maintain active direct access pathways to manufacturers for complex firmware calibrations.

Service Level Commitments:

  • ✔ Single Number Dispatch: Speak directly to dispatch managers who route certified field staff.
  • ✔ Stocked Spares: Extensive local warehouse housing rollers, sensors, boards, and cartridges.
  • ✔ Preventative SLA: Routine scheduled cleaning, dust removal, and sensor test routines.

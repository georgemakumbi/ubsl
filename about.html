About Our Company

Establishing trust in currency handling through engineering precision and responsive technical support since 2005.

Our Origins

Why Unique Business Systems Was Founded

Unique Business Systems Limited (UBSL) was founded in 2005 as an engineering-focused service provider. The company was born out of a clear realization by its founders that financial institutions, cash-in-transit (CIT) firms, and other money-handling organizations lacked prompt, high-quality technical support for their machinery.

While hardware sales were common, technical support and routine maintenance were largely ignored by the market. We set out to change this by making service delivery, spare parts availability, and engineering excellence the core pillars of our business.

"We don't just sell technology. We partner with cash-intensive organizations to manage throughput, reduce cost, and protect liquidity."

Our Mission

To provide quality goods and services to our clients, ensuring promptness, reliability, and engineering efficiency in every single service delivery.

Our Vision

To become the leading regional center for money handling and business solutions across Uganda and the wider Great Lakes region.

Corporate Mandate

Our Service Objectives

  • ✓ Operational Efficiency: Increase customer throughput and scale of operations via high-efficiency cash sorting.
  • ✓ Document Security: Secure cheques, drafts, and business instruments against fraudulent manipulation.
  • ✓ Global Standards: Continuously upgrade employee and technical staff training to match global currency and security marks.
What We Do

Core Engineering Services

Unique Business Systems Limited delivers end-to-end technical services for currency processing. We ensure maximum machinery uptime for Uganda's financial infrastructure.

Sales & Supply

Direct supply of certified coin counters, ATM fit note sorters, and currency detectors with manufacturer warranties.

Preventative SLA

Scheduled preventative maintenance visits, sensor calibrations, dust cleaning, and wear-and-tear prevention.

Emergency Repairs

Software Upgrades

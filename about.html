Our Origins

Why Unique Business Systems Limited Was Founded

Unique Business Systems Limited (UBSL) is an engineering-focused company which founded in 2005. The company was originated from clear realization that there was a need to provide efficient and advanced currency handling machinery as well as on-time maintenance and service to financial institutions, cash-in-transit (CIT) firms and other money-handling institutions.

While hardware sales were common, technical support and routine maintenance was largely ignored by the market. We set out to change this by not only supplying machinery but also providing reliable maintenance and service, timely software upgrades and spare part availabiity in order to ensure uninterrupted operations and reduce costs from procuring new machines.

"We don't just provide technology. We partner with currency handling institutions to manage throughput, reduce cost and protect liquidity."

Our Mission

To provide quality goods and services to our clients, ensuring promptness, reliability, and engineering efficiency in every single service delivery.

Our Vision

To become the leading regional service provider of currency handling solutions across Uganda and the African Great Lakes region.
